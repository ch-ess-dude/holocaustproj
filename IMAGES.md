# IMAGES.md — Image Slot Guide

Every image slot in the site currently shows a labelled placeholder. This file
lists each slot, describes what image belongs there, and recommends a specific
public-domain or properly licensed source with a suggested attribution caption.

**Critical note on content:** Do not use graphic atrocity imagery — mass graves,
bodies, or scenes of extreme physical suffering. Favour portraits, documented
places, archival documents, and memorial photography. All sources below are
appropriate for an educational context.

---

## Timeline Images

### 1933 — The Nazi Seizure of Power
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Hitler's appointment as Chancellor, Berlin, January 1933
- **Recommended image:** Photograph of the torchlight parade on 30 January 1933 in Berlin,
  OR a street-level photograph showing SA boycott signs on Jewish businesses, April 1933.
- **Source:** German Federal Archives (Bundesarchiv), Bild 102 series
- **URL:** https://www.bild.bundesarchiv.de (search "Hitler Reichskanzler 1933")
- **Attribution caption:** Bundesarchiv, Bild 102-xxxxx / Photographer / CC-BY-SA 3.0 DE

---

### 1935 — The Nuremberg Laws
- **Slot label:** Document: Original text of the Nuremberg Laws, 1935
- **Recommended image:** Photograph of the official Reich Law Gazette (Reichsgesetzblatt)
  page showing the Nuremberg Laws text, OR the front page of the decree.
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons (public domain — German government document, pre-1945)
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org (search "Nuremberg Laws Reich Law Gazette")
- **Attribution caption:** Reich Law Gazette, 16 September 1935. Public domain.

---

### 1938 — Kristallnacht
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Burned synagogue or destroyed shopfront, November 1938
- **Recommended image:** Exterior photograph of a burned or damaged synagogue in Germany,
  November 1938. USHMM holds several such images marked as cleared for educational use.
- **Source:** United States Holocaust Memorial Museum (USHMM) Photo Archives
- **URL:** https://collections.ushmm.org (search "Kristallnacht synagogue")
- **Attribution caption:** USHMM, courtesy of [donor name]. Photo: [photographer if known].

---

### 1939 — Invasion of Poland & the Ghettos
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Warsaw Ghetto street scene, c. 1940–41
- **Recommended image:** Street scene from the Warsaw Ghetto showing civilians, taken by a
  German soldier or official photographer, c. 1940–41. Many are in USHMM and Yad Vashem.
- **Source:** USHMM Photo Archives or Yad Vashem Photo Archive
- **URL:** https://collections.ushmm.org or https://photos.yadvashem.org
- **Attribution caption:** USHMM / Yad Vashem. [Specific credit per selected image.]

### 1941 — Mass Shootings in the East
- **Slot label:** Memorial photograph: Babi Yar memorial site, Kyiv
- **Recommended image:** Contemporary photograph of the Babi Yar National Memorial Reserve
  in Kyiv, Ukraine — the ravine itself or the memorial menorah sculpture. This avoids
  depicting the massacre and instead honours the site as a place of memory.
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons (contemporary photography, freely licensed)
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org (search "Babi Yar memorial")
- **Attribution caption:** [Photographer], CC BY-SA [version], via Wikimedia Commons.

---

### 1942 — The Wannsee Conference & Industrial Murder
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Wannsee House (Villa am Wannsee), Berlin — now a memorial
- **Recommended image:** Exterior photograph of the Villa am Wannsee, now the House of
  the Wannsee Conference memorial and educational site.
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons or the House of the Wannsee Conference (ghwk.de)
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org (search "Wannsee Conference villa")
- **Attribution caption:** House of the Wannsee Conference. [Photographer], [license].

---

### 1943 — The Warsaw Ghetto Uprising
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Stroop Report photograph showing ghetto fighters, Warsaw, 1943
- **Recommended image:** The famous photograph from the Stroop Report showing civilians
  and fighters being led from the ghetto. This image is public domain (German government,
  wartime) and is widely reproduced in educational contexts. It does NOT show graphic violence.
- **Source:** Bundesarchiv / Wikimedia Commons
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org (search "Stroop Report Warsaw Ghetto Uprising")
- **Attribution caption:** Bundesarchiv, Bild 183-41636-0002 / Juergen Stroop Report /
  Public domain.
- **Note:** Select the image showing the group being escorted, not combat images.

---

### 1944 — The Deportation of Hungarian Jews
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Hungarian Jews arriving at Auschwitz-Birkenau, May 1944
- **Recommended image:** Photograph from the "Auschwitz Album" showing Hungarian Jewish
  families on the platform at Birkenau, before selection. This is one of the only known
  photographic records made inside the camp during the deportations. It is public domain
  and held by Yad Vashem.
- **Source:** Yad Vashem, the Auschwitz Album
- **URL:** https://www.yadvashem.org/yv/en/exhibitions/album_auschwitz/
- **Attribution caption:** The Auschwitz Album, Yad Vashem Photo Archive. Public domain.
- **Note:** Choose an image showing families on the platform, not the selection process itself.

---

### 1945 — Liberation
- **Slot label:** Photograph: Soviet soldiers at the gates of Auschwitz, January 1945
- **Recommended image:** Photograph of Soviet soldiers or Red Army medics at Auschwitz
  at the time of liberation, January 27 1945. USHMM holds several.
- **Source:** USHMM Photo Archives
- **URL:** https://collections.ushmm.org (search "Auschwitz liberation 1945")
- **Attribution caption:** USHMM. [Specific credit per selected image.]

---

### 1945–46 — The Nuremberg Trials
- **Slot label:** Photograph: The Nuremberg Trials courtroom, 1945–46
- **Recommended image:** Wide-angle photograph of the Nuremberg courtroom showing the
  defendants' dock, defence counsel, and Allied judges. Several US Army Signal Corps
  photographs are public domain.
- **Source:** US National Archives / Wikimedia Commons
- **URL:** https://commons.wikimedia.org (search "Nuremberg trials courtroom")
- **Attribution caption:** US Army Signal Corps / National Archives and Records Administration.
  Public domain.

## General Attribution Guidance

1. **USHMM:** Each image page at collections.ushmm.org shows specific credit and rights
   information. Always copy the exact credit line shown.
2. **Yad Vashem:** Contact the Photo Archive directly for educational use permissions:
   https://www.yadvashem.org/archive/photo-archive.html
3. **Bundesarchiv:** Most images are CC-BY-SA 3.0 DE. The required attribution format is:
   `Bundesarchiv, Bild [number] / [photographer name or "unknown"] / CC-BY-SA 3.0 DE`
4. **Wikimedia Commons:** Always check the individual file's licence. Public domain (PD-old,
   PD-USGov, PD-Bundesarchiv) requires no payment but still requires attribution.
5. **Do not hotlink** to any of these archives — download images and host them locally in
   `public/images/` so the site works offline.
